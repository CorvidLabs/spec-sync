# Requirements

- The system SHALL provide the draft, approved, implementing, verifying, accepted, and archived states without bypasses.
- The system SHALL bind approvals and verification evidence to deterministic content and invalidate stale evidence.
- The system SHALL validate semantic requirement/spec deltas and the effective contract against source code.
- The system SHALL support guided adoption, Spec Kit/OpenSpec imports, and Claude/Cursor/Codex/Gemini artifacts.
- The system SHALL preserve existing projects as opt-in while enabling SDD for newly initialized projects.
