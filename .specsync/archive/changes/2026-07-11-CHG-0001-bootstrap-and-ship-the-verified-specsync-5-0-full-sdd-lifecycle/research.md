# Research

Spec Kit contributes structured feature phases and agent commands. OpenSpec contributes proposal-oriented change workspaces and deltas. SpecSync 5.0 combines those strengths with deterministic interviews, stable requirement identity, digest-bound approval gates, executable verification, bidirectional code-contract validation, concurrent-change conflict detection, and portable adoption.
