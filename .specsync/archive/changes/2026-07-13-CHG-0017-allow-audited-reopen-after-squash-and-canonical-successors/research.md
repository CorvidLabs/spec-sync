# Research

Git ancestry alone cannot recognize a verification commit removed by squash. Comparing the entire workspace to the remote also fails after legitimate successor governance. Committed `state.json` history provides auditable acceptance evidence, while later accepted/canonical records provide bounded successor coverage through existing affected-spec and affected-path matching.
