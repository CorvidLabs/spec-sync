# Requirements

The normative statements are the semantic deltas. They follow the sentences in `hi/plan.md`.

- REQ-change-104 — a draft is asked which existing lessons or principles constrain it, only when some exist, and "none" is a complete answer. Changing that answer changes the agreed scope.
- REQ-change-105 — a selected plan cannot be approved until it says what will be done, what will not, the steps, the risks, and which constraints are honored. A change that did not select a plan does not grow one.
- REQ-change-106 — opening a change shows the lessons already written for its modules, and the project's principles when there are any. A scaffold is not knowledge. The structured result carries the same briefing.
- REQ-cmd-change-018 — finalize and ship show where the lesson bundle is, and the merge instruction does not wait on copying lessons into the spec.
- REQ-cmd-rules-002 — `specsync rules` names the principles file, and a lesson becomes a standing rule only when a person writes it there.
