### REQUIREMENT REQ-cmd-init-008

The `.specsync/.gitignore` that initialization writes SHALL contain no U+2013 (en dash) or U+2014 (em dash).

Acceptance Criteria
- The header line reads ``# spec-sync 5.0: generated by `specsync init` ``.
- The ignored entries are unchanged.
- An existing `.specsync/.gitignore` is not rewritten.
