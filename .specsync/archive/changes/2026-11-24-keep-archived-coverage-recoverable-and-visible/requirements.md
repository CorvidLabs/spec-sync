# Requirements

A project maintainer can reopen and re-finalize a provisionally covered archive without manually deleting its prior generated receipt. A maintainer looking at an archive can distinguish absent coverage from a present invalid receipt and identify the file to restore. The operator can still trust the Git-backed archive evidence and the rollback behavior when auxiliary coverage is unusable.
